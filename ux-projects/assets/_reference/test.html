Flow strip — reference

What the component looks like populated. Eight screens, so the group overflows and the arrows and connector appear; two screens carry real explanation text, so the comment bubble is shown.

Runs from file:// with no server. Manifest is test-manifest.js, a classic script assigning window.FLOW_MANIFEST_REFERENCE.

What this demonstrates

  • Arrows — left and right edges, vertically centred, overlapping the outermost tiles. Advance by exactly four. Prev disabled at the start, next at the end. No looping.
  • Connector — one only, above the last visible tile of page one, pointing right past its edge. Gone on page two, back when you page down. Tight 2 4 dashes at 2.5px, hard 90° elbow.
  • Frame — wide 4 6 dashes at 60% opacity, with the use-case label on the border. Container versus signal.
  • Bubble — expand screen 1 or 3. Anchored above the top-right corner, tail crossing the screen edge, 200ms pop from the tail, max 380px. Screens 2 and 4–8 have explanation: null and show no bubble.
  • Expand — height-fitted to 85vh. Esc closes and returns focus to the tile. Focus is trapped while open.
  • No scrollbar — none at any width; touch swipe still scrolls the track.
  • Breakpoints — 4 across at ≥1100px, 2 at 700–1099px, 1 below.
